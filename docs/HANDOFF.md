- Sounds (Oct 27): a move's sound is cued by `store.js` (`fx.cue`) and claimed by the board (`fx.claim`), which times it to the motion: a draw as the card leaves, a card set down at the settle, the foundation chime at the landing, the paper flip as the face comes round. Auto-complete chimes rise a little per card. `flip.wav` and `found_1..3.wav` are synthesised by `tools/make_sounds.py` (CC0, ours). The Motion Lab has a Sounds panel with the same files.

## Known gaps and risks
- The drawn decks were checked in Chromium on Linux only. Reading Room has faceless engraved court figures (double-ended, cut on the diagonal, items per suit as in the English pattern); Thorn & Star and Centre Ice keep emblem courts by decision.
- Nothing tested in Safari or on a real iPhone. Motion speed on older phones is unknown. Victory sequences were checked from frames, not watched.
- Draw 3 has a smaller solver budget for the dead-end check, so more dead ends may go unflagged.
- The coach may now be too quiet (the early-foundation rule almost never fires on winning games).
- Deals handed out look easier than random ones (small simulation).
- `privacy.html` (for an App Store release) still has a placeholder email and is not in this repository.

## Ideas not built

## Useful simulations (not in the repo)
Simulated players (a simple careful bot, a random bot) were used to measure how often the coach fires on winning lines and whether lost games end. They are easy to rebuild from `engine.js`, `solver.js` and `critic.js`.

## How to test on a phone
1. After `publish.sh`, wait about a minute, then on the iPhone open the home-screen app, leave it and come back: it checks for the update on return. Settings > About > Version must show the stamp `publish.sh` printed.
2. Check one game in each deck, light and dark (Settings > Appearance > Mode), portrait and landscape.
3. Sound: Settings > Sound and haptics, Effects volume Subtle. Listen for the paper flip on a reveal and the chime on a foundation.
4. Accessibility: iPhone Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver on, then move a card by double-tapping; Reduce Motion on (Accessibility > Motion) and watch cards fade instead of fly; Text size Larger in the game's settings.
5. Never delete and re-add the home-screen icon to force an update: it can erase saved games and stats.
